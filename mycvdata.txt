Culinary Culture: A Global Exploration of Health and Diversity in Cuisine
First Author, IEEE Xplore ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11491234
• Built a fusion architecture that combines a transformer-based text encoder for recipe ingredients with a CNN branch
for dish images.
• Refined a dataset of 4,986 recipes, consolidating over 76 regional cuisine labels into 30 country-level classes to
improve semantic coherence and class balance.
• Achieved 81 percent accuracy in cuisine classification and 79 percent in diet prediction. Both results were higher
than the unimodal baselines.
BanglaRobustNet: A Hybrid Denoising-Attention Architecture for Robust Bangla Speech Recognition
Second Author, IEEE Xplore ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11491383
• Implemented a hybrid ASR framework on a Wav2Vec-BERT backbone, adding a diffusion-based denoising module
and a contextual cross-attention module conditioned on speaker embeddings.
• Trained end to end with a composite objective of CTC loss, phonetic consistency and speaker alignment.
• Reduced Word Error Rate and Character Error Rate on Mozilla Common Voice Bangla and augmented noisy speech,
improving on both Wav2Vec-BERT and Whisper baselines.
TAXCHAIN: A Blockchain-Based Income Tax Management System for Bangladesh with NID Integration
Second Author, IEEE Xplore ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11491821
• Designed a permissioned blockchain linking National ID and Taxpayer ID to automate Tax Deduction at Source.
• Wrote smart contract logic for progressive tax slabs and annual reconciliation on an immutable ledger.
